New here?

Here's what a Sunday looks like.

Grab a coffee, find a seat, and join us for worship and teaching from the Bible. The service runs about 90 minutes. Kids check in at the foyer and go straight to Children's Church for their own worship and Bible lesson.

Not sure what to wear or where to park? We've answered the common questions so you can walk in feeling at home.
