Missions

Making Christ known, near and far.

We support missionaries serving around the world, and we go ourselves on short-term mission trips. Here's how you can pray, give and go.

Missionaries we support

Our mission partners

Oaxaca, Mexico · Wycliffe Bible Translators

The Merchant Family

Josh and Emily Merchant serve with Wycliffe Bible Translators in Oaxaca, where they teach at Oaxaca Christian School. The school serves the families of missionaries and Bible translators working across Mexico, so the work of bringing God's Word to people in their own language can keep going.

Give

Java & Bali, Indonesia · Pioneers

The Torna Family

David Torna and his family lead Know Indo, a church-planting ministry in Indonesia. In a remote Java village where no missionary had ever lived, they dedicated their first building for worship this year. In Bali, they care for families in a student sponsorship program, most from Hindu backgrounds, and are seeing people come to faith.

Tanzania & Malawi · Steve and Susan Vinton

Village Schools International

Village Schools partners with rural villages in East Africa to build Christian secondary schools, where students get a quality education and hear the gospel. Villages invite them in and do the hard work of building alongside them.

Our Thanksgiving Harvest Offering funds scholarships so more girls can go to school.
