Crossroads Counseling Center

A quiet, restorative place to receive help and bring about real change in everyday life.

Our ministry

We counsel adults, youth, and children as young as eight, working with individuals, couples and families.

Crossroads accepts many insurance plans and has a sliding fee schedule to make our services readily available.

If you are at a crossroads in your life, we would love to help you find your way.

Get in touch

Call the church office at (530) 885-4385 or email info@coolchurch.org, and we'll connect you with a counselor.

Meet our counselors

Dave Robertson, LCSW

Dave earned a BA in Psychology from UC Davis and a Master of Social Work from CSU Sacramento, and has been a licensed clinical social worker since 1985.

Kim Robertson, LCSW

Kim earned her BA in Sociology and Master of Social Work from CSU Sacramento, and has been a clinical social worker since 1998.

Alongside Biblical, Christ-centered principles, Dave and Kim use appropriate therapeutic approaches including cognitive, behavioral and communication-based methods. Both are trained in EMDR to provide relief from symptoms of trauma.
