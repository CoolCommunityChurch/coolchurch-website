Classes & groups

The best way to get to know people at Cool Community is to join something. Here's what meets each week and how to jump in.

Adult Sunday School

  • WhenSundays, 9:00–10:00 am
  • WhereAt the church, before the 10:30 service
  • WhoAll adults
  • JoinJust come. No sign-up needed.

We walk through curriculum and study the Word together, a chance to go deeper in faith and closer to one another.

Foundations of the Faith

  • WhenSundays, 9:00–10:00 am
  • WhereAt the church, taught by Dr. Drew Buell
  • WhoAdults who want a firm grasp of what the Bible teaches
  • JoinSign up at the Welcome Desk. Books are $10.

Home Groups

  • WhenDifferent nights of the week
  • WhereIn homes around Cool and the Divide
  • WhoAdults, couples and families
  • JoinSign up online and we'll help you find a group near you.

Home Groups make the big Sunday morning feel a little smaller: a place to share life, study Scripture and pray together.

Join a Home Group

Empty Nesters (55+)

  • WhoAdults 55 and up in a new season of life
  • JoinCall the church office at (530) 885-4385 or check "Classes and Bible studies" on the Connect Card.

Not "senior adults," and not done. A group for those who want meaningful worship, real community, and to keep serving others for God's glory.

Women's Bible Study Fellowship

  • WhenFridays, 9:30–11:30 am
  • WhereAt the church
  • WhoWomen of all ages
  • JoinRegister online, or call the church office at (530) 885-4385.

This year's study, From Guilt to Grace, walks through the book of Romans.

Register for BSF

GriefShare

  • WhenThursdays, 4:30–6:00 pm
  • WhereAt the church
  • WhoAnyone grieving the loss of a loved one
  • JoinSign up through GriefShare, or call the office.

A caring group that walks alongside you through one of life's hardest seasons. You don't have to grieve alone.

GriefShare sign-up

Mommy and Me

  • WhenThursdays, 9:30–10:30 am
  • WhereIn the church sanctuary
  • WhoMoms and their little ones
  • JoinJust come. No registration needed.
